computer scince

Computer science is the study of computers, programming, algorithms, data, and technology used to solve real-world problems. It helps students understand how software and computer systems work. Computer science also includes programming, problem-solving, databases, artificial intelligence, networking, and cybersecurity, which are important areas in today’s rapidly growing world of technology.


web development

Web development is the process of creating websites and web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern development tools. It allows developers to build attractive, responsive, and user-friendly websites. Web development also includes designing layouts, adding interactive features, improving performance, and creating websites that work smoothly on different devices and screen sizes.
